How to measure

Chest: Grab your best-fitting tee, lay it flat, and measure across the chest just below the sleeves. Match it to the chart.

Length: Measure from the highest point of the shoulder (next to the collar) straight down to the bottom hem.

Fit tips

  • True to size — order your usual size for a classic fit
  • Between sizes? Size up for a relaxed fit, down for a closer fit
  • Heavyweight cotton holds its shape — minimal shrinkage when washed cold and tumble dried low
