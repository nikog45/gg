# Early Miles Club

**Domain:** earlymilesclub.com (verified unregistered via Verisign RDAP, 2026-07-23)
**Backups (also unregistered):** quietmilesclub.com · firstlightrunclub.com · basepaceclub.com · beforehoursrunclub.com

The third brand in the playbook. Slow Pour Society owns the slow morning coffee. BLKFLD owns the
minimal wardrobe. Early Miles Club owns the hour before the city wakes up.

---

## Positioning

**One line:** A badge for the people who run before anyone's watching.

**The niche:** Run club culture — but specifically the *easy miles* side of it. Not race day, not
PRs, not carbon plates. The 6 a.m. loop, zone 2 pace, the run that nobody posts about. Every
running brand sells speed; nobody owns the quiet miles that make up 80% of every runner's life.

**Anti-positioning (the SPS formula):**
- SPS: "Some people rush their coffee. You're not one of them."
- EMC: "Some people sleep in. You're not one of them."

Not a performance brand. No moisture-wicking claims, no marginal gains. This is what you wear
*after* the run, to the coffee, to the office — the thing that lets other members spot you.

## Audience

Urban runners, 20–35, Europe-first (EUR pricing). In a run club or run-club-adjacent. They track
their easy pace, know what zone 2 means, run before work, and identify as runners even on rest
days. Overlaps heavily with the SPS customer — same person, different ritual. Cross-promote.

## The Badge (not a logo)

**The Club Crest:** a hand-drawn crest — rising sun over an empty road, ringed with
"EARLY MILES CLUB · OUT BEFORE THE CITY". Same logic as the SPS pour-over seal: it reads as
membership, not merch. Chest placement, single hit, no big back prints at launch.

## Voice

Calm, dry, a little conspiratorial. Short sentences. Never hype, never "crush your goals."
Talks about the ritual (alarm, laces, empty streets, light coming up) — not performance.
Sibling of the SPS voice; a cousin of BLKFLD's restraint.

**Words we use:** miles, easy, early, quiet, club, members, ritual, light, streets
**Words we never use:** grind, beast, crush, PR, gains, elite, performance

## Visual system

- **Palette:** off-white (#F5F2EC), near-black (#141414), sunrise orange accent (#E8622C) used sparingly
- **Type:** editorial serif or clean grotesk for headings, quiet sans for body — match SPS energy, not BLKFLD's
- **Imagery:** empty streets at dawn, streetlights still on, flat light, no action shots of sprinting
