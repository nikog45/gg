## REFUND & RETURNS POLICY

Every Label Tee is pressed to order, one at a time — but that never affects your legal
rights. Here's how cancellations, returns, and refunds work.

### Your 14-day right to cancel
If you are a consumer in the UK or EU, you have the right to cancel your order for any
reason within 14 days of receiving your items, under the Consumer Contracts Regulations
2013. To cancel, e-mail us at frozoaservice@hotmail.com within that period — a clear
statement that you want to cancel is enough. You then have a further 14 days to send the
items back.

### Returns
- E-mail us first at frozoaservice@hotmail.com with your order number — we'll confirm the
  correct returns address. (Please don't post anything to our registered office.)
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition.
- Return postage is at your cost unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong thing.
- We recommend using a tracked service — the items are your responsibility until they
  reach us.

### Refunds
We refund within 14 days of receiving the returned items (or of you providing proof of
return postage, whichever is earlier), to your original payment method. The refund covers
the price of the items plus standard delivery; we may reduce the refund if items show
handling beyond what a shop fitting would involve.

### Faulty, damaged, or wrong items
If your order arrives damaged, has a print defect, or isn't what you ordered, e-mail us a
photo and your order number and we'll send a replacement or a full refund — including any
postage — whichever you prefer. No need to return the faulty item unless we ask.

### Exchanges
Because every tee is pressed to order, we don't offer direct size swaps — cancel or return
the original as above and place a new order for the size you need.

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.

## SHIPPING POLICY

Every piece is pressed to order — like all good records. Here's what that means for
delivery times.

### Production time
Allow 2–5 business days for your order to be printed before it ships. You'll receive a
dispatch e-mail with a tracking link the moment it's on the move.

### Estimated delivery times (after production)
- United Kingdom: 2–4 business days
- Europe: 5–10 business days
- Rest of the world: 10–20 business days

These are estimates, not guarantees — carriers occasionally have their own ideas. If your
order hasn't arrived within 30 days of ordering, e-mail us at frozoaservice@hotmail.com
and we'll replace it or refund you.

### Shipping costs
Calculated at checkout based on destination, and shown before you pay.

### Customs and import duties
Orders shipped outside the United Kingdom may be subject to import taxes, duties, or
handling fees charged by the destination country. These are set by your local authorities,
are the recipient's responsibility, and are not included in our prices or shipping charges.

### Addresses
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout — each item is printed and
dispatched for the address you give us. If a parcel is returned to sender because of an
incomplete or incorrect address, we may need to charge for re-delivery. Spotted a mistake
right after ordering? E-mail us immediately and we'll do our best to catch it before
production.
