# Long Play Society

**Domain:** longplaysociety.com (verified unregistered via Verisign RDAP, 2026-07-23)
**Backups (also unregistered):** fullsideclub.com · needledropsociety.com · firstpressingclub.com

Brand #4. Slow Pour Society owns the slow coffee morning. BLKFLD owns the minimal wardrobe.
Early Miles Club owns the hour before the city wakes. Long Play Society owns the evening the
phone stays face-down and a record plays all the way through.

---

## Positioning

**One line:** A badge for the people who listen to the whole side.

**The niche:** Vinyl and listening culture — turntable owners, pressing nerds, listening-bar
regulars. Not DJ culture, not merch for a band: the *ritual of listening*. Sleeve out, needle
down, sit down. The anti-shuffle.

**Anti-positioning (the house formula):**
- SPS: "Some people rush their coffee. You're not one of them."
- EMC: "Some people sleep in. You're not one of them."
- LPS: "Some people shuffle. You're not one of them."

## Audience

Urban 20–40, Europe-first (EUR pricing). Owns a turntable and a growing shelf. Knows what a
first pressing is, has opinions about reissues, sits through Side A without touching anything.
Heavy overlap with the SPS customer — same person, later in the day. Cross-promote all three.

## The Badge (not a logo)

**The Label:** a circular record center-label design — hand-set type ringed around a center
hole, like a 12" label from an old pressing plant. Reads: "LONG PLAY SOCIETY" around the top
arc, "PLAY IT ALL THE WAY THROUGH" around the bottom, "33⅓ RPM · SIDE A" as the fine print.
It reads as a record label, not a logo. Chest placement, single hit.

**The Label Tee — Long Play Society** · €34,95

## Voice

Unhurried, warm, record-shop-counter dry. Talks about the ritual (sleeve, dust brush, needle
drop, the run-out groove) — never about gear specs or audiophile numbers. No snobbery: the
enemy is shuffle, not Spotify.

**Words we use:** side, pressing, sleeve, groove, needle, listen, society, all the way through
**Words we never use:** audiophile, lossless, rig, setup, superior, hi-res

## Visual system

- **Palette:** cream (#F4EFE6), near-black (#171310), amber accent (#C98A2B) used sparingly
- **Type:** hand-set / grotesk headings with an old-label feel, quiet sans body
- **Imagery:** sleeves leaning against a wall, a turntable in warm lamp light, hands lowering a
  needle, record-shop crates — evening warmth, never neon, never club photography
