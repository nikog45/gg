## Positioning

**One line:** A chant for the people who go hot, then cold, then again.

**The niche:** Sauna and cold plunge culture — urban saunas, plunge clubs, contrast-therapy
regulars. The ritual is rounds: heat until you can't, dip until you gasp, sit until you're
human, repeat. Wellness brands sell serenity; nobody sells the *club* feeling of the sauna
bench and the gasp at the plunge barrel. That's ours.

**Anti-positioning (the house formula, with a twist):**
- "Some people stay lukewarm. You're not one of them."

The enemy is lukewarm — lukewarm showers, lukewarm plans, lukewarm people.

## Audience

Urban 20–40, Europe-first. Goes to a sauna or plunge club weekly, owns a sauna hat or wants
one, evangelizes contrast therapy at parties. Overlaps with the EMC runner (post-run sauna)
— cross-promote.

## The Badge (not a logo)

**The Split Badge:** a circle cut horizontally. Top half: rising steam waves in ember orange.
Bottom half: water ripples in ice blue. Ringed text: "SWEAT DIP REPEAT" top arc, "ROUND
AFTER ROUND" bottom arc. Reads like a bathhouse token, not a logo. Chest placement, single hit.

## Voice

Playful, crisp, a little dared-you energy. Short punchy lines that alternate hot and cold.
Never woo-woo wellness speak — no "journey", no "biohacking", no cortisol talk. The humor of
people grinning through a freezing barrel.

**Words we use:** rounds, dip, steam, gasp, bench, barrel, löyly, lukewarm (as an insult)
**Words we never use:** biohack, cortisol, optimize, wellness journey, ice bath protocol

## Visual system

- **Palette (duality):** cream (#F7F3EE), charcoal ink (#1C1C1C), ember orange (#E4572E),
  ice blue (#7FB6C9). The whole identity is the hot/cold split — layouts divide in half,
  hot content warm, cold content cool.
- **Type:** bold crisp geometric for headings (Archivo Black energy), clean sans body.
  No grunge, no groove — Nordic clean with playful copy doing the personality work.
- **Imagery:** steam on cedar, frost on a plunge barrel, red-cheeked people laughing in
  towels, winter lake jetties. Warm/cold split compositions. Joyful, never spa-serene.
