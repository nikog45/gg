# Slow Pour Society — Launch Kit

A specialty-coffee community apparel brand. Merch as a membership badge for
people who take their coffee seriously and their mornings slowly.

**Concept:** Concept B (hobby/community) · **Niche:** specialty coffee ·
**Fulfillment:** print-on-demand (Printful / Printify / Gelato) — zero inventory.

> **Compliance status:** clean on all Whop policies. Original artwork only, no
> claims, ordinary retail goods, no age-gating needed. Guardrails repeated at the
> end. Verify the `.com` domain + USPTO Class 25 (apparel) availability for the
> name before committing.

---

## 1. Brand positioning

**One-liner:** *Gear for people who slow down for a better cup.*

**Positioning statement:** Slow Pour Society is a coffee-culture apparel brand
for home brewers and café regulars who treat coffee as a ritual, not a
transaction. We don't sell beans — we sell belonging. Every piece is a quiet
signal to the other people who own a scale and know their grinder settings.

**Who it's for:** 24–45, home-brew and third-wave café enthusiasts, own a pour-
over setup or espresso machine, spend on gear and single-origin bags, active on
coffee TikTok/Reddit/IG.

**Why they buy:** identity and belonging. The graphic is the badge.

**Brand voice:** warm, unhurried, a little in-joke-y for people who "get it."
Never hypey. Think a knowledgeable barista friend, not a salesperson.

## 3. Visual identity

### Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Espresso | `#3B2417` | Text, logo, dark garments |
| Secondary | Kraft / Crema | `#D8C3A5` | Backgrounds, tags, packaging |
| Accent | Terracotta | `#C1663A` | Buttons, highlights, badges |
| Support | Sage | `#7C8471` | Secondary graphics, calm accents |
| Base light | Oat | `#F3EDE3` | Site background, negative space |
| Base dark | Roast Black | `#1E1712` | Footer, contrast blocks |

Warm, earthy, "third-wave café" — reads premium and calm, not loud.

### Typography

- **Display / headlines:** *Fraunces* (Google Fonts) — a warm, slightly retro
  serif with character; perfect for a "society/club" feel.
- **Alt display (condensed badges):** *Anton* or *Bebas Neue* for badge lockups.
- **Body / UI:** *Inter* or *Work Sans* (Google Fonts) — clean, highly legible.

All are open-source (SIL Open Font License) — free for commercial use, no
licensing risk.

### Logo direction

A circular **"society seal/badge"** lockup: "SLOW POUR SOCIETY" wrapping a
central pour-over dripper or a coffee cherry/bean icon, with an "EST. 2026" and a
small tagline ribbon. Works as embroidery, sticker, and stamp. Keep a simplified
wordmark version for small placements (hat, care tags).

Each brief = concept, placement, colors, and copy. Give these to a designer or an

## 5. Starter product lineup + margin table

POD base costs are approximate (Printful-class) and vary by color/size/provider —
verify live before launch. Retail set for healthy ~55–70% gross margins typical
of POD apparel.

| SKU | Product | Est. POD base | Retail | Gross profit | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SPS-TEE | Heavyweight cotton tee (Bella+Canvas 3001) | ~$13 | **$32** | ~$19 | ~59% |
| SPS-HAT | Embroidered dad/trucker hat | ~$18 | **$28** | ~$10 | ~36% |
| SPS-MUG | Ceramic mug 11oz | ~$8 | **$18** | ~$10 | ~56% |
| SPS-HOOD | Pullover hoodie (back print) | ~$30 | **$58** | ~$28 | ~48% |
| SPS-STK | Sticker pack (3–4) | ~$3 | **$9** | ~$6 | ~67% |
| SPS-TUM | Insulated tumbler | ~$21 | **$32** | ~$11 | ~34% |
| SPS-BND | Member bundle (tee + mug + stickers) | ~$24 | **$52** | ~$28 | ~54% |

**Pricing notes**
- Tee is the volume/hero SKU — keep it at a psychologically clean $32.
- Hats/tumblers carry thinner POD margins; treat them as range-fillers and
  bundle-boosters, not primary profit drivers.
- The **bundle** raises average order value and improves blended margin — feature
  it prominently.
- Add free-shipping threshold at ~$50 to push bundle/multi-item orders.

**Illustrative unit economics (hero tee at $32):**
Retail $32 − POD base ~$13 − payment fee ~$1.20 = **~$17.80 contribution** before
ad spend. Keep customer-acquisition cost under that on first order; profit
compounds on repeat community buyers.

## 6. Launch sequence (suggested)

1. Lock name/domain + trademark check (Class 25).
2. Set up POD provider + connect to Shopify.
3. Produce Designs 001–003 first (hero tee, instructional tee, hoodie).
4. Shoot/mock product images in warm café settings (or POD mockups to start).
5. Publish store + policy pages (Refund/Terms/Privacy/Shipping).
6. Soft-launch to coffee communities (Reddit r/coffee, IG, TikTok) with the
   "Join the Society" hook.
7. Later: introduce a fixed-term paid community ("The Society") — keep it a
   defined membership period, never "lifetime," per Whop rules.

---

## 7. Compliance guardrails (recap)

- **Original artwork only** — no third-party logos, characters, or parody of
  protected marks (counterfeit/infringing goods are prohibited).
- **No claims** — apparel makes no health/earnings/results promises.
- **Accurate listings** — real materials, sizing, total price before purchase.
- **Policies live before launch** — Refund, Shipping, Terms, Privacy.
- **If you add a paid community later** — fixed access term, no "lifetime,"
  genuine testimonials only, disclose any incentivized reviews.
