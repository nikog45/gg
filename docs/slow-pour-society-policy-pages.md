# Slow Pour Society — Policy Pages (Drafts)

Drafts for all six Shopify policy pages (Settings → Policies). Replace every
`[BRACKETED]` placeholder with your real business details before publishing.

> **Not legal advice.** These are working drafts aligned with EU/German consumer
> law basics and the compliance baseline in `whop-compliance-guide.md`. Have a
> lawyer (or a service like Trusted Shops / IT-Recht Kanzlei, standard in
> Germany) review before launch — especially the Impressum and Widerrufsrecht,
> where errors can trigger Abmahnungen (formal cease-and-desist letters).
>
> **Language note:** if you market to German consumers, German-language versions
> of these pages are strongly advisable (and for the Impressum, expected).
> These drafts are in English to match the storefront copy; translate as needed.

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within **14 days** without
giving any reason. The withdrawal period expires 14 days from the day on which
you, or a third party named by you (other than the carrier), takes physical
possession of the goods.

To exercise this right, you must inform us —

[LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]
[ADDRESS]
[SUPPORT EMAIL]

— of your decision to withdraw by an unequivocal statement (e.g. a letter sent
by post or an email). You may use the model withdrawal form below, but it is
not obligatory. To meet the deadline, it is sufficient to send your
communication before the withdrawal period expires.

**Effects of withdrawal:** if you withdraw, we shall reimburse all payments
received from you, including delivery costs (except supplementary costs from
choosing a delivery type other than our least expensive standard delivery),
without undue delay and no later than 14 days from the day we are informed of
your decision. We will use the same means of payment you used for the initial
transaction unless expressly agreed otherwise; you will not incur any fees for
the reimbursement. We may withhold reimbursement until we have received the
goods back or you have supplied evidence of having sent them back, whichever is
earliest.

You shall send back the goods without undue delay and no later than 14 days
from the day you communicate your withdrawal. You are only liable for any
diminished value of the goods resulting from handling other than what is
necessary to establish their nature, characteristics and functioning.

## 2. Datenschutzerklärung — Privacy Policy

Shopify shows this as **"Automatisiert"** — use Shopify's auto-generated privacy
policy as the base (Settings → Policies → Privacy Policy → "Create from
template"), because it stays current with Shopify's actual data processing.
Then verify/add these sections so it matches what the store really does:

- **Data controller:** [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME], [ADDRESS], [EMAIL]
- **Processors actually in use:** Shopify (hosting/checkout), your POD provider
  ([Printful/Printify/Gelato] — order name + shipping address are shared with
  them to fulfill orders), Judge.me (reviews), your email marketing tool
  (newsletter), and any analytics/ads pixels you install (Google, Meta, TikTok)
- **Newsletter:** double opt-in (standard in Germany), purpose, and a working
  unsubscribe in every email
- **Cookie consent:** a consent banner that blocks non-essential
  cookies/pixels until accepted (required for EU visitors — use a consent app,
  e.g. Pandectes, Consentmo, or Shopify's Customer Privacy settings)
- **Rights notice:** access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection,
  and the right to complain to a supervisory authority; for Germany name your
  competent Landesdatenschutzbehörde
- **Data retention:** orders kept per statutory commercial/tax retention
  periods (Germany: 6–10 years under HGB/AO)

original works owned by [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] and may not be reproduced or
resold without permission.

**9. Age.** Purchases require you to be 18+ or have the consent of a parent or
guardian.

**10. Liability.** We are liable without limitation for intent and gross
negligence and under mandatory law (e.g. product liability). For slight
negligence, we are liable only for breach of essential contractual obligations,
limited to the foreseeable, contract-typical damage.

**11. Dispute resolution.** The EU Commission's platform for online dispute
resolution: https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr. We are not obliged and not
willing to participate in dispute resolution before a consumer arbitration
board [adjust if you choose to participate].
